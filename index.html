VvAVES

The slightest drop in one part of an ocean can lead to a wave in another.

A pixel world simulator, realistic enough to be informative and free enough for fantasy and real experiments. One procedural chemistry builds everything: the landscape, the weather, fire, rock and the first plants. Every pixel is simulated.

Try it in your browser

Drop something in

A tiny taste of the idea, nothing like the full game: pick a material and draw. Lava meets water and turns to stone, sand sinks in water, water spreads.

Click and drag (or touch) to draw

Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit

Download

Both builds are a single zip. Unzip anywhere and double-click the Start file. Nothing is installed.

First run

How to start it

Unzip

Right-click the zip, Extract All, open the folder.

Start

Double-click the Start file. A black window opens and the world opens in your browser.

Windows warning

The builds aren't signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. Close the black window to stop.
